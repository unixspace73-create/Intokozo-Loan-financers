Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our loans

What is the minimum loan amount I can apply for?

The minimum loan amount is R100. We offer flexible loan amounts to suit your needs.

How much interest do you charge?

We charge 40% interest on all loans. This is a once-off payment, meaning you pay the loan amount plus 40% interest in a single payment.

Can I apply if I receive a SASSA grant?

Yes! We welcome SASSA grant recipients. To qualify, you need grant income for 2 or more children. You'll need your pension ID, SASSA card, and either your latest cash withdrawal slip or 3 months of bank statements.

How long does the approval process take?

We pride ourselves on fast approval. Most applications are processed quickly, often on the same day, provided all required documents are complete and in order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including cash, bank transfers, and debit orders. Our loan structure requires a once-off payment of the principal plus interest.

Do I need to be employed to apply?

For our standard loan, yes, you need proof of employment (latest payslip). However, SASSA grant recipients can apply with their grant income if they receive grants for 2 or more children.

Where is your branch located?

We are located at 670 Tax Rank Street, Kgapane. You can visit us during our operating hours or contact us via WhatsApp or email for more information.

Is my personal information safe?

Absolutely. We take data protection seriously and comply with POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act). Your information is kept confidential and secure.
